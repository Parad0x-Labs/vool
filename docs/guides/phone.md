# Use VOOL from your phone

VOOL Companion is VOOL's app for Android phones. It talks to VOOL on your own computer: your
chats, files and wallet stay there, and the phone does only what you allow. It works only with
VOOL installed on your computer, and phone access stays off until you switch it on.

There is no iPhone app yet.

## 1. Get the app

On your Android phone, open the
[latest VOOL release](https://github.com/Parad0x-Labs/vool/releases/latest) and download the
VOOL Companion file, the one that ends in `.apk`. Open it to install. Android asks whether your
browser may install apps: allow it for this install.

## 2. Switch it on, on your computer

Open **Settings → Phone and chat apps**. Choose your home or private network in the list, switch
on **Use VOOL from my phone**, then press **Pair a phone**. A code appears on the screen.

## 3. Scan the code with your phone

Open VOOL Companion and press **Scan pairing QR**. Allow the camera when the phone asks, and point
it at the code on your computer. If you can't scan, enter the computer address, pairing id and
8-character code instead. If the app says the code has expired, press **Pair a phone** on your
computer again.

## 4. Choose what the phone may do

When the phone is connected, your computer says **Your phone is connected. Choose what it may do,
then press Done.** Chat, chat history, attachments and alerts are on once you press **Done**.

* **Online lookups:** **Look things up online** or **Off**. Lookups use free web sources only.
  Your paid models and keys are only used from the computer itself.
* **Files:** **None**, **Only the folders I choose** (the phone can open and change files there,
  and nothing else) or **Everything on this computer** (files, apps, commands and your accounts).
* **Payments:** **Off** or **On, with limits**. Set up a wallet on your computer first. Payments
  start in the phone's Wallet tab and are always approved on your computer, and your wallet PIN
  confirms the limits.

Press **Done**, and VOOL says **Saved. Your phone is ready.** To change a single permission later,
open **Advanced** under your phone.

On a Mac, if VOOL says the folders are not open to it yet, allow VOOL in **System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Files and Folders**, then press **Done** again.

## Away from home

At home, the phone reaches your computer over your home network. To use VOOL over mobile data
too, switch on **Also use VOOL away from home** on the same page, then pair or scan the code
again. The phone then reaches your computer through a private route. There is nothing else to set
up, and messages stay sealed. The first connection away from home takes a little longer. Your
computer must be on, with VOOL open.

If the private route cannot start, your home network still works. Switch **Also use VOOL away
from home** off and on to try again.

## Stop using the phone

Switch off **Use VOOL from my phone**. That closes phone access.
