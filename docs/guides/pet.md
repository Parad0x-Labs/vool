# The pet

VOOL has a small painted pet that sits in the chat window, or on your desktop in the Mac app.
It shows what VOOL is doing for you. It is only for show: hiding or moving it never stops a
task, a chat or VOOL itself.

## Choose your pet

Press `⌘ /` (`Ctrl+/` on Linux), or choose **Pet: choose a character…** in the command
palette. The **Choose your pet** panel shows a live preview and ten pets to **Pick a pet**:

| Pet | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Morrow Beetle | rune-shell beetle |
| Clockwork Raven | brass clockwork raven |
| Stone Golem | mossy crystal golem |
| Tide Wisp | diving-helmet octopus |
| Spark · Lamplighter | brass lantern bee |
| Rascal · Courier | steam courier tank |
| Prime · Keykeeper | golden key knight |
| Prism · Lanternfin | crystal shard fish |
| Veil · Duskmoth | many-eyed dusk moth |
| Ember · Kiln | living furnace |

**Show pet** in the same panel turns the pet **On** or **Off**.

## Move it

* Drag the pet anywhere inside the chat window.
* In the Mac app, drag it past the edge of the window to put it on your desktop. It floats
  above ordinary windows, but never above a dialog you have to answer.
* Right-click the pet for its menu: **Dock inside VOOL**, **Character Lab…**, **Toggle edge
  snapping**, **Hide pet**, the motion setting and the pet choice.

If the pet is lost on the desktop, open **Settings → Appearance → Desktop pet** and use
**Return to app** or **Reset position**.

## Hide and show

Choose **Hide pet** in the pet's menu, in the command palette (**Pet: hide**) or in
**Settings → Appearance → Desktop pet**. The same button then reads **Show pet** and brings it
back.

## What the bubble says

While VOOL works, a bubble above the pet shows two lines taken from what is really happening,
never made-up phrases:

1. **The task**, in a few words from your own message (a greeting has no task line).
2. **The current step**, in the same words as the working card in the chat, for example
   *Reading report.pdf*. It changes when the work changes.

* When VOOL is waiting for your approval, the bubble says what the approval is for, and stays
  until you answer.
* When the answer is done, it says **Done** with how long the turn took, then goes quiet after
  a few seconds.
* If a turn stopped or failed, it says so and stays up until something changes.

## Good to know

* In a browser tab, the pet stays inside the chat page; the desktop pet needs the VOOL app
  window.
* If your computer asks for less motion, the pet moves less. The motion setting in its menu
  overrides that.
