# What's new in 0.8

VOOL 0.8 is about trust: you can see where each answer came from, what VOOL did, and what it
cost. It also reaches further: a real browser window, chats from Telegram and Discord, and a
way to send files to another VOOL. Everything here is off or asks you first until you choose
otherwise. The full list of fixes is in the [changelog](../help/changelog.md).

## See what VOOL did

* **Where each answer was answered.** The line under an answer says whether a model on your
  computer or a cloud service wrote it. [Check your VOOL](check-your-vool.md)
* **Receipts for actions.** When VOOL says it did something, such as saving a memory, the
  receipt reads it back so you can see it really happened. [Receipts](../concepts/receipts.md)
* **VOOL Doctor.** One page in Settings checks every part of VOOL and says in plain words what
  is wrong and what to do. [Check your VOOL](check-your-vool.md)
* **Today.** A new chat opens with a short box of what is coming up today.
  [Check your VOOL](check-your-vool.md)
* **AI spending you can read.** Settings shows what paid models cost, billed or estimated, and
  lets you set your own limits. [Check your VOOL](check-your-vool.md)
* **Report this answer.** Tell VOOL an answer was wrong, out of date, remembered the wrong thing
  or did the wrong action. [Bug reporting and privacy](../trust/bug-reporting.md)
* **A clearer first run.** Setup walks you through a few short steps.
  [First run](../getting-started/first-run.md)
* **See what leaves your computer.** Before files or remembered information first go to a
  cloud model, VOOL shows the whole request. You can **Send only these parts**, **Replace names
  and details**, or **Try this with another model**. Nothing is sent until you choose.
  [See what leaves your computer](what-leaves-your-computer.md)

## Reach further

* **VOOL Browser.** Browse in your own browser window, open a private window, and send web
  traffic through Tor or your own VPN with one button. VOOL works on web pages only with your
  OK. It uses the Google Chrome or Chromium browser already on your computer. [VOOL Browser](browser.md)
* **Drop.** Send a note and a file, sealed, to another person's VOOL through a folder you both
  already sync. Drop is an early test version. [Send with Drop](drop.md)
* **Telegram and Discord.** Make a bot in a few steps and chat with your VOOL from either app.
  You choose what the bot may do; anything that needs approval is approved on your computer.
  [Chat from Telegram and Discord](telegram-and-discord.md)
* **Use VOOL from your phone.** Pair the VOOL Companion app for Android from
  **Settings → Phone and chat apps** and choose what the phone may do. Chat with VOOL, take
  photos with the private camera, open the VOOL Vault, get alerts, see the wallet, approve or
  deny requests with **Allow once** or **Deny**, and reach VOOL away from home if you switch it
  on. [Use VOOL from your phone](phone.md)
* **X drafts, ratings and results.** Rate VOOL's X drafts so later ones fit your voice, and ask
  how your own posts did. Every post still needs your approval. [X drafts, ratings and
  results](x-social.md)

## Memory you control

* **Forget leaves no words behind.** After "forget X", the list that keeps the words from
  coming back holds only scrambled fingerprints, never the words themselves.
  [Memory](../concepts/memory.md)
* **The chat keeps up.** Follow-up questions are answered with your recent messages in view.
* **New run modes.** The model menu offers **Computer + web** (no cloud AI, web lookups still
  work) and **Offline** (nothing leaves this computer). [Local and
  cloud](../concepts/local-and-cloud.md)
* **My things.** Keep receipts, manuals and details about your equipment on this computer.
  [My things](my-things.md)
* **My routines.** Show VOOL a folder task once, check the exact list of changes, and run it
  again when you choose. [My routines](routines.md)
* **Lend a job to a friend.** Your Mac can do a small job for a friend's VOOL over Drop: a
  photo contact sheet, a clean PDF or a trimmed recording. An early test version, off until you
  switch it on. [Lend a job to a friend](lend-a-job.md)

## Skills and plugins

* **Anyone can publish plugins in the Skill Codex**, next to skills. A plugin never overrides
  VOOL's rules.
* **Ratings, badges and listings.** Ratings come from people who installed the add-on. A badge
  holds for one exact version and runs out. Installed cards show **Checked on this device**.
* **A daily check for removed add-ons**, on by default.
* **Other people's skills stay in their place.** They are used only when you ask for them and can
  only narrow the tools VOOL offers. A skill VOOL wrote by itself needs your **Allow**.

Read [Skills and plugins](skills-and-plugins.md) and [Add-on security](addon-security.md).

## Wallet

The wallet is still off until you turn it on.

* **Named wallets:** Main, Everyday and Burner, with rename, make main and delete.
* **Backups** sealed with a backup password in your computer's secure store or on a USB stick,
  to get back in after a forgotten PIN or bring back a deleted wallet.
* **A Skills & plugins wallet** with its own limit for each plugin.

Read [Wallet](wallet.md).

## Everything else

* **Ten new pets**, painted and animated, whose bubble shows what VOOL is actually doing.
  [The pet](pet.md)
* **PDFs open on Linux**, read in a locked-down sandbox.
* **Switches in Settings read plainly**: On with a green light, Off with a red light.
