# More in VOOL

## Your rules

Rules are things VOOL must always or never do, kept in a numbered list. Say them in any chat:

* "Add this to global rules: never use the word synergy" applies everywhere.
* "Add to project rules: reply in Greek" applies to every chat in this chat's project.
* "Add to chat rules: no emoji" applies to this chat only.
* "What are my rules" shows the list with numbers.
* "Remove rule 2" takes that rule off.

VOOL says a rule is saved only after it really is. Rules stay after a restart.

Good to know:

* Words a rule bans are blanked out of VOOL's replies.
* If a rule bans a word, VOOL refuses to make a Git commit, tag or push that contains it.
* A ban adds up across levels: a chat rule cannot lift a global ban. Remove the global rule
  for that.
* Rules can only be added or removed on this computer's own VOOL.

## The Activity panel

Press **Activity** in the chat header (`⌘ J` on a Mac, `Ctrl+J` on Linux) to see what VOOL
did while it answered. The panel has these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | Each turn, with the tools VOOL used |
| **Steps** | The steps of the current task |
| **Changes** and **Files** | The files VOOL changed, by change and by file |
| **Tests** | Test runs |
| **Preview** | A preview of the result, when there is one |
| **Agents** | Helpers VOOL started, and whether they are running or done |
| **Council** | A council review, when you asked for one |
| **History** | Past tasks, with a way to build a bug report from a failed one |
| **Records** | The signed receipts of what happened |

**Scope** picks what the panel covers: **Current chat**, **Current project** or **All
activity**. The filter box narrows the list to events, tools, file names or models, and
**Copy all** copies everything shown in the tab.

**Settings → Advanced → Extra pages → Open the activity page** opens a step-by-step view of
what VOOL is doing in its own window.

## Plan a social media campaign

When the built-in **Social content manager** skill helped with an answer, a card appears
under it: **Save this campaign?** Fill in **Goal**, **Who it is for** and **Where (for example
X, LinkedIn)**, then press **Save campaign**, or **Not now**. The campaign is kept on this
computer. Nothing is posted anywhere.

## The phone app's Settings tab

Besides pairing and permissions, the Settings tab of the VOOL phone app has:

* **Screen lock**: the app locks after 1, 5 or 15 minutes without use.
* **Smaller files** → **Make files smaller before sending**: turn on **Compress** to shrink
  photos and videos before they go to your computer, and pick **Best**, **Balanced**,
  **Smallest** or **Custom**. Off sends the originals. With it on, **Remove location** and
  **Remove camera model** under **Privacy** take that information out of the file.
* Shortcuts for the private camera and VOOL Vault: **Add to quick settings** where the phone
  allows it, and, on Android, **VOOL Camera on the home screen** and **VOOL Vault on the home
  screen**. The Vault shortcut still opens only after you unlock VOOL.

See [Use VOOL from your phone](phone.md) for pairing and the rest of the app.
