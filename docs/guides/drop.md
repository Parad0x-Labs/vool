# Send with Drop

Drop sends a short note and one file from your VOOL to another person's VOOL, or to another of
your own computers. Everything is locked before it leaves, and it travels through a folder you
both already sync, such as a shared Dropbox or iCloud Drive folder. There is no VOOL server and
no account.

**Drop is an early test version.** VOOL says so on the page: **Early test version. Do not use it
for private messages yet.** Its encryption has not had an independent review.

Drop is off until you switch it on.

## What you need

* VOOL on both computers (macOS or Linux).
* A folder that both of you have and that syncs between you: a shared Dropbox, iCloud Drive or
  similar folder. It must already exist and belong to you. Only locked files go into it.

## 1. Switch it on

Do this on both computers.

1. Open **Settings → Backup & restore → Send with Drop**.
2. Tick **Enable Drop and make this VOOL folder private**.
3. Under **Shared folder**, press **Choose folder** and pick the folder you both sync.
4. Press **Save**.

## 2. Make your card

Under **You**, type your name and press **Save name**. Your card appears, with your **safety
number** and three words, your key tag (for example `river-lamp-stone`).

Press **Copy card** and send it to the other person any way you like: email, a message, a chat.
Your card is not a secret.

## 3. Add the other person

1. Under **People**, paste their card into **Their card**. VOOL shows the card's three words and
   safety number.
2. Check the name under **Name for this person**.
3. Press **Add person**.

When you have both added each other, the person shows as **Ready**. Until then they show as
**Has not added your card yet**.

## 4. Check the safety number once

The safety number is twenty digits made from the other person's key. Read it to each other once,
on a call or in person. If it matches, press **We compared the number: mark as verified**.

Until you do, the person shows **Not verified**. You can still send, but you are not yet sure
the card came from them.

The three words next to every name tell your people apart at a glance. Nobody can choose them,
so two people with similar names never look the same.

## 5. Send a note and a file

1. Under **Send to**, choose the person.
2. Write a note if you like, and choose one file.
3. Press **Send file**.

VOOL says **Sent to** *name*. **It arrives when their VOOL is open.** Once their VOOL has the whole
file, your Drop shows **Delivered.**

You can also ask in chat, for example "send the invoice in my Downloads to Anna with Drop". VOOL
asks for your OK every time, and tells you whether anything was sent. Drop only sends to other
computers, never to a phone.

## 6. Receive

While VOOL is open it checks for new Drops by itself. You can also press **Check for Drops**.

A new Drop shows who sent it, the note, and the file's name and size. While pieces are still
coming in it says **Arriving**. When it is complete:

* Press **Save to Downloads** to keep the file. Saving never replaces a file that is already
  there.
* Press **Show in folder** to see it.

Until you save it, the file stays locked inside VOOL. VOOL never follows instructions written in
what it receives, and no AI model reads a received Drop.

## Remove a person

Press **Remove** next to the person, then press again to confirm. Their Drops are deleted, and
anything they send afterwards is refused.

## Good to know

* One file at a time, up to 10 MB. The note can be up to 4,096 characters (fewer for letters
  outside the Latin alphabet).
* A Drop expires after seven days.
* Both of you need a folder you both sync. There is no other way to deliver yet.
* If you send several files in a row, the other person's VOOL must be opened once before it can
  take more.
* VOOL refuses a card under a name you already use, or one that looks like it, when the card is a
  different VOOL. That refusal is the warning: check with the person on a call.
* Names are unique on your computer only. Always check the three words and the safety number.
* Drop does not protect copies the other person makes outside VOOL, or their backups.
* **One-time invite, without adding the person** is an older way to send a single Drop. Most
  people never need it.
* The sender can ask the receiver's VOOL to check that the file arrived unchanged. The receiver
  decides with **Approve and run file check** or **Decline**. Turn this on with **Allow approved
  file checks**.
