# Contacts

Contacts keeps the people and services you save once: their email addresses, phone numbers,
messaging names and wallet addresses. Email drafts, payment proposals and invitations use the
exact entry you pick, so you do not have to type an address again. Nothing in Contacts sends a
message or pays anyone.

Your contacts stay on this computer. Because they feed payments and messages, changing a saved
contact asks for your PIN or password first.

## Open Contacts

* Press **Contacts** at the bottom of the sidebar, or
* open the command palette and choose **Contacts…**.

The list is on the left. Type in **Search by name, alias or address** to find someone. Choose a
contact to see every saved entry.

## Add a contact

1. In Contacts, press **Add contact**.
2. Type the **Name**, and choose **Person** or **Service**.
3. Add **Other names (comma separated)** and **Notes** if you like.
4. Under **Entries**, choose the kind (**Email**, **Phone**, **Messaging** or
   **Wallet address**) and type the address. Use **+ Add an entry** or **+ Add a wallet** for
   more. For a wallet, choose the chain this person uses.
5. Press **Save contact**.

A new contact is saved at once. If the name is the same as, or looks like, a contact you
already saved, it waits for your PIN or password instead, so a lookalike name cannot slip in.

You can also ask VOOL in chat to save someone.

## Change or delete a contact

1. Choose the contact and press **Edit**.
2. Make your changes and press **Review changes**.
3. VOOL shows the saved contact as it is and the exact change. Enter your PIN or password and
   press **Confirm this change**, or press **Cancel this change**.

**Delete contact** works the same way: you review the deletion and confirm it.

If you have not set a PIN or password yet, press **Protection**, then **Set up a PIN or
password**. Until then, changes wait and nothing is changed. Keep the recovery code VOOL shows
you: it resets a forgotten PIN or password.

Changes that wait for you are listed under **Pending changes**. Nothing opens by itself.

## Import contacts

1. In Contacts, press **Import**.
2. Under **Import from**, choose **Apple Contacts**, **Google Contacts** or
   **Microsoft Outlook**.
3. Tick **Allow VOOL to read the contacts in this account for a preview. Nothing is imported
   until I choose.** and press **Connect**.
4. Press **Preview** next to the source. VOOL reads the account once and shows what it found,
   with a note on each person: new, already saved, the same name as a saved contact, or a name
   that looks like one.
5. Choose the people you want and press the import button.

Good to know:

* Apple Contacts works on a Mac. macOS asks for access on the first preview.
* Google and Microsoft need a verified connection to that account first. The import page says
  so if one is missing.
* VOOL never changes anything in the account it reads from.
* Imported entries are marked **Imported, not verified** until you press **Confirm entry**.
* Adding entries to a contact you already saved, or importing a name that looks like a saved
  one, waits for your PIN or password.
* If the source could not be read, nothing is imported.
* **Remove source** stops future previews. You choose whether to keep the imported entries or
  erase the ones still marked imported.

## Put a contact into a message

* In a contact, press **Use in chat** next to an entry, or
* open the command palette, choose **Insert a saved contact…**, type in **Find a contact** and
  pick the entry.

The exact address goes into your message box. **Copy** copies it instead.

## Good to know

* When a change cannot go through, VOOL says so and tells you what was kept, for example
  "Nothing was saved." or "... changed after this was reviewed; review it again. Nothing
  changed."
* If your PIN or password was accepted but saving did not finish, press **Save it again**.
* A change you leave waiting expires after a while. Make it again to review it.
* A message or payment that points to a contact you later deleted says so. It is never
  quietly sent to someone else.
* Only a limited number of changes can wait at once.

## Why it works this way

A mistyped payment address can lose money for good. Reviewing each change means no chat
request, bulk import or old window can change who "Alice" is without you confirming the exact
result.
