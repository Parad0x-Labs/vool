# X drafts, ratings and results

Connect your own X account first and set your monthly limits; see
[X integration](x-integration.md). Reads use your X account's own API allowance.

## Drafts and approval

Ask VOOL to draft a post, a reply or a quote. Anything that would act on your X account is
only **proposed**. It waits for your approval in Settings, and a proposal made in a chat also
appears under the answer that made it. Approving in either place sends exactly the action you
saw, once; declining sends nothing. A proposal that expired needs a new one. If X's answer
gets lost, the action shows *Unclear if it was sent*: check X before you try again.

## Rate drafts

Beside a waiting or sent post, choose a rating from 1 to 10 and, if you like, add a comment.
When you next ask for an X draft, VOOL shows the drafting model a few of your highest and
lowest ratings for that project (or your general ones). Ratings guide drafting; they do not
change your model or promise a post will do well.

A rating of 9 or 10 offers **Use as a voice sample**. Saving the rating alone does not add a
voice sample.

VOOL saves a rating only when you give the score yourself in that message. It never guesses
one.

The ratings panel lets you pause the guidance, forget one rating, or reset a project's
ratings. Forgetting a rating also removes a voice sample added from it. It does not erase
copies of that rating already in your chat history.

## Results of your posts

Ask how your own posts did, for example "how did my posts do this week?". VOOL reads the
numbers X returns for each post and saves when it looked and how old the post was. A number X
does not give is shown as *not available*. One reading is a snapshot; compare posts only at
the same age. A post you deleted loses its saved readings on the next read. When X limits
requests, VOOL stops and tells you when X says you can try again.

## Comments you have not answered

Ask "which comments on my post haven't I answered?". VOOL reads recent comments on your own
post and leaves out the ones you already answered or already have a reply waiting for. If it
cannot see enough of your own replies to be sure, it says so instead of marking comments as
unanswered. Draft a separate reply for each comment; every reply needs its own approval.

## Skills that use X

A skill you installed can name the X and note tools it needs. It can only be offered public X
reads, your own X results and unanswered comments, VOOL notes, ratings, proposal status, web
reads (still refused while web lookups are off) and the three proposal tools. A skill is never
given your direct messages, contacts, files, Apple Notes, mail, calendar or messages; those
reach a chat only when you ask for them. Naming a tool gives a skill no permission, connection
or spending of its own.

## Good to know

* There is no automatic posting. Every action waits for your approval.
* Approving X actions from your phone is not part of this version.
