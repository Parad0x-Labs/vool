# Data handling

## The two states

**LOCAL** — everything stays on this computer.

**CLOUD** — the selected context is sent directly to the selected provider.

The indicator in the interface shows which state is active before you send anything.

## What VOOL does not do

* It does not proxy your prompts through Parad0x Labs infrastructure.
* It does not upload your workspace.
* It does not send your key anywhere except to the provider it belongs to.

## OpenRouter specifically

Your OpenRouter account remains yours. VOOL sends requests directly from this computer
using your key. Parad0x does not proxy your prompts or pay for your usage. Genuine VOOL
usage may appear in VOOL's public aggregate OpenRouter statistics, but individual prompts
and identities are not published.

## Telemetry

Product telemetry is off by default and is a setting you control. See
[Configuration](../reference/configuration.md).

## Your provider's terms apply

In cloud mode your data reaches your provider under **their** retention and training
terms, not VOOL's. Read the terms of whichever provider you select.
